## 2. Color Palette

Every post MUST use colors from this palette. No random blues, reds, or stock-template colors.

### Primary Colors

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Bond Navy** | `#0f172a` | Primary background for 80% of posts. This is the signature dark-mode look. |
| **Bond Lime** | `#ccff00` | Primary accent. Headlines, stats, CTA buttons, highlighted words. The "pop" color. |
| **White** | `#ffffff` | Body text on dark backgrounds. Secondary background for light-mode posts. |

### Secondary Accent Colors (use sparingly, max 1 per post)

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Bond Purple** | `#d946ef` | Stats, badges, secondary highlights. Use for "before" states or problem framing. |
| **Bond Cyan** | `#06b6d4` | Data points, icons, tech-related content, AI features. |
| **Bond Orange** | `#f97316` | Urgency, warnings, "stop doing this" content. |
| **Bond Pink** | `#ec4899` | Engagement hooks, questions, polls. |

### Color Application Rules

1. **Default Post**: Bond Navy background + Bond Lime accent + White text.
2. **Alternate Light Post** (max 1 in every 5): White/light-gray background + Bond Navy text + Bond Lime accent.
3. **Never** use gradients with more than 2 brand colors.
4. **Never** use colors outside this palette. No random greens, blues, or reds.

## 3. Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Headlines** | Inter Black | 900 | ALL CAPS or Title Case. Must be large and dominant (40-60% of the canvas). |
| **Sub-headlines** | Inter Bold | 700 | Sentence case. Used under the main headline for context. |
| **Body text** | Inter Regular | 400 | Used sparingly — Instagram is visual-first. Max 2-3 short lines. |
| **Handwriting accents** | Nothing You Could Do | 400 | Used for annotations, arrows, "handwritten" notes on images. Sparingly. |
| **Stats / Numbers** | Inter Black | 900 | Oversized (takes up 30-50% of canvas). Always in Bond Lime on dark background. |

### Typography Rules

1. **Hierarchy is king.** Every post must have a clear visual hierarchy: one dominant element (headline or stat), one supporting element (sub-text), one CTA.
2. **No more than 3 font sizes** per single-image post.
3. **Line spacing**: Tight for headlines (line-height: 1.0-1.1), relaxed for body (line-height: 1.4-1.5).
4. **Letter spacing**: Headlines should use tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`). Body text uses normal tracking.

---

### 5.2 — Carousel Post (1080×1350 portrait 4:5, 5-10 slides)

This is the **workhorse format**. 60-70% of posts should be carousels. Using 4:5 portrait (not square) maximizes screen real estate in the feed.

**Slide Structure:**

| Slide | Purpose | Design |
|---|---|---|
| **Slide 1 (Hook)** | Stop the scroll. Bold headline, no body text. | Bond Navy bg, Bond Lime headline, "AI" logo top-left. Swipe arrow bottom-right. |
| **Slides 2-8 (Content)** | One idea per slide. Short text + visual. | Bond Navy bg, White text, Lime accents for emphasis. Numbered if sequential. |
| **Slide 9 (Summary)** | Recap key takeaways in bullet form. | Bond Navy bg, Lime bullet points, White text. |
| **Slide 10 (CTA)** | Drive action: "Save this", "Follow @invictus__ai", "DM us" | Bond Lime bg (inverted!), Bond Navy text. Logo centered. WhatsApp link or CTA. |

**Carousel Rules:**
1. Each slide must be readable in **under 3 seconds**.
2. Max 40-50 words per slide.
3. Use numbered lists, icons, or visual separators — never walls of text.
4. The CTA slide ALWAYS uses the **inverted color scheme** (Lime bg, Navy text) to create visual disruption.
5. **Pro tip:** Add music to carousels — this pushes posts into the Reels feed algorithm for extra reach.

## 7. Visual Element Library

### Icons & Decorators

- **Arrows**: Hand-drawn style arrows (using the handwriting font aesthetic) pointing to key stats or CTAs.
- **Underlines**: Lime-colored brush-stroke underlines under key words.
- **Circles/Highlights**: Lime-colored circle around important stats or words (hand-drawn feel).
- **Dividers**: Thin horizontal lines in Bond Lime or 20% opacity white.
- **Number badges**: Lime circles with Bond Navy numbers inside (for numbered lists/carousels).

### Background Patterns

| Pattern | When to Use |
|---|---|
| **Solid Bond Navy** | Default. 70% of posts. |
| **Subtle grid overlay** (matches website bg) | Educational carousels, adds a "tech" feel. Use the same 40px grid from the website at 3-5% opacity. |
| **Gradient: Navy → slightly lighter navy** | Feature showcases, product demos. |
| **Solid White** | Inverted CTA slides, testimonials, light-mode variety posts. |

### Photography Rules

- **Never use stock photos of smiling generic people.**
- Real clinic photos (with permission), screenshots of our dashboards, and generated premium illustrations are acceptable.
- If using a photo, always apply a Bond Navy overlay (60-80% opacity) so text remains readable.

## 8. Caption & Hashtag Framework

### Caption Structure (for every post)

```
[HOOK — 1 line that stops the scroll. Bold claim or question.]

[BODY — 3-5 short paragraphs. One idea per paragraph. Use line breaks generously.]

[CTA — Clear ask: Save, Share, Follow, DM, or click link in bio.]

---

[HASHTAGS — placed below a line break separator]
```

### Caption Rules

1. **First line is everything.** It must be a scroll-stopper. Use a stat, a bold claim, or a direct question.
2. **No fluff.** Every sentence must earn its place.
3. **Use emojis strategically** (max 3-5 per caption). Don't overload.
4. **End with a clear CTA.** "Save this for later 🔖" or "DM 'GROWTH' to get started."

## 11. Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ DO

- Keep every post on-brand (Navy + Lime).
- Make the first carousel slide irresistible to swipe.
- Use real data and specific numbers wherever possible.
- End every post with a clear CTA.
- Maintain the "premium tech company" aesthetic at all times.
- Post consistently (4x/week minimum).

### ❌ DON'T

- Use stock photos of generic smiling people.
- Use random colors outside the brand palette.
- Post without a caption or with a lazy 1-line caption.
- Use more than 2 fonts in a single post.
- Post blurry, pixelated, or poorly cropped images.
- Copy competitor aesthetics — our identity is unique.
- Use Comic Sans, Papyrus, or any novelty fonts.
- Post content that attacks or undermines our own services.
