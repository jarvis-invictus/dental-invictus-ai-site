## Frequently Asked Questions
  
  <FAQAccordion>
    <FAQItem question="Do patients get frustrated talking to an AI bot?">
      Not if it's built correctly. Patients get frustrated by old 'rule-based' bots that don't understand their questions. Modern LLM-based bots understand natural language, typos, and voice notes. Plus, patients prefer an instant AI response at 10 PM over waiting 12 hours for a human reply.
    </FAQItem>

    <FAQItem question="Can the chatbot hand off the conversation to my receptionist?">
      Yes! The best dental chatbots feature "Human Handoff." If a patient asks a complex clinical question or requests to speak to the doctor, the AI instantly pauses and pings your front desk staff to take over the WhatsApp chat seamlessly.
    </FAQItem>

    <FAQItem question="Do I need a new WhatsApp number for this?">
      You can use your existing clinic WhatsApp number by upgrading it to the official WhatsApp Business API. This allows the AI to connect directly to Meta's servers for fast, reliable automation.
    </FAQItem>
  </FAQAccordion>
