## What Is a Dental AI Chatbot?

A dental AI chatbot is a software program trained on your clinic's information — treatments, pricing, hours, location, doctor profiles, and FAQs — that automatically responds to patient messages on WhatsApp, your website, or Instagram DMs.

It is not a scripted phone menu from 2010. Modern AI chatbots powered by large language models (LLMs) understand natural language, handle follow-up questions, and respond in a conversational, human-sounding way.

## What to Look For in a Dental Chatbot Service

When evaluating providers, ask these questions:

### ✅ Is it trained on dental-specific knowledge?
A generic chatbot will not know the difference between composite bonding and ceramic veneers, will not understand common patient concerns about root canals, and will give generic responses that don't build trust. Your chatbot needs to be trained on your specific services and dental terminology.

### ✅ Can it book appointments directly?
Many chatbots just collect a name and phone number and say "we'll call you back." That is not a chatbot — that is a digital business card. A real appointment-booking chatbot integrates with your calendar (Google Calendar, or your practice management software) and confirms real time slots.

### ✅ Does it hand off to a human gracefully?
When a patient asks something the chatbot cannot handle — a complex clinical question, a complaint, or a request to speak to the doctor — the handoff must be seamless. The chatbot should notify the front desk with full context of the conversation.

### ✅ Does it work in Hindi and regional languages?
A significant percentage of your patients communicate primarily in Hindi or a regional language. An English-only chatbot will fail with a large segment of Indian patients.

### ✅ Is there analytics?
You should be able to see: how many conversations happened, how many appointments were booked, what questions patients asked most, and where patients dropped off. This data helps you improve the bot over time.

## Setting Up Your Dental Chatbot: What to Prepare

Before setting up a chatbot, prepare this information:

**Clinic Information**
- Clinic name, address, landmark, Google Maps link
- Opening and closing times (including weekends)
- Doctor name(s) and qualifications
- Contact number and WhatsApp number

**Services and Pricing**
- List of all treatments offered
- Price ranges for common treatments (exact prices optional, but ranges help)
- Any currently running offers or discounts

**FAQ Bank**
Write answers to the 20 questions patients ask most often. Examples:
- "Is the RCT painful?"
- "Do you accept insurance?"
- "Is there parking available?"
- "What is the cost of dental implants?"
- "Do you treat children?"

**Appointment Protocol**
- What information does the front desk need to confirm a booking? (Name, phone, preferred time, treatment needed)
- What is your cancellation policy?
- What should patients bring on their first visit?

The more detail you provide upfront, the more accurate and useful your chatbot will be from day one.

## FAQ

### Will patients know they are talking to a bot?

It depends on how you configure it. Some clinics name the bot (e.g., "Smile Assistant") and are transparent. Others prefer it to appear as a clinic team member. Transparency usually works best — patients just want fast answers.

### Can the chatbot handle patient complaints?

For simple queries, yes. For clinical complaints, the chatbot should immediately flag the conversation to a human and provide a warm handoff message.

### What happens when my clinic is closed?

This is exactly where chatbots shine. When your clinic is closed, the chatbot continues responding, collects appointment requests, and queues them for review when the clinic opens.
