## When Practo Actually Helps

Practo is not worthless — it genuinely serves certain purposes well.

<StepList>
  <AnimatedStep number="1" title="Credibility Verification">
    Patients who hear of you via referral often search Practo to verify credentials. A complete free profile acts as a credibility signal.
  </AnimatedStep>
  <AnimatedStep number="2" title="Specialty Discovery">
    In major metros (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore), patients actively search for specialists (Orthodontists, Oral Surgeons). Prime might generate meaningful leads here.

## When Practo Falls Short

<Callout type="warning" title="The Competitor Trap">
  This is Practo's biggest problem: **when a patient clicks on your Practo profile, they see your competitors listed right next to you.** Practo's business model depends on patients comparing options.
</Callout>

### You Build Practo's Authority, Not Yours
When Practo ranks on Google for "dentist near me in Pune," they get the click — not you. Every review and booking builds <Highlight color="cyan">Practo's domain authority</Highlight>. Your own website, by contrast, builds **your** authority. Over 2–3 years, a well-maintained website will outrank Practo.

### No WhatsApp — India's #1 Conversion Channel
Practo does not integrate with WhatsApp. In India, where 73% of patients prefer WhatsApp communication, this is a significant conversion bottleneck.

## The Best Strategy: Use Both — But Invest Right

**Free Practo profile:** Always yes. Takes 30 minutes, zero cost, adds credibility.

**Practo Prime/Boost:** Only if you are in a major metro, practicing a specialist service, have consistently good reviews (4.5+), and can measure actual bookings coming from Practo.

**Own website:** Always yes. This is your #1 digital asset. Build it properly, optimize it for local SEO, connect it to WhatsApp, and create treatment content monthly. Over 24 months, it will outperform any Practo subscription.

## FAQ

### Can I rank on Google without my own website?

You can rank in the Google Map Pack using only your Google Business Profile. However, for organic search results and targeting specific treatment keywords, you need your own website.

### Practo says they get millions of visitors. Why am I getting few leads?

Practo's traffic is divided across hundreds of thousands of listings. Your visibility depends on your plan, reviews, specialty, and competing clinics. In Tier 2 cities, individual listings often receive fewer than 100 impressions per month.

### Is there a cheaper alternative to Practo for listings?

Yes — and most are free. Justdial, Lybrate, Sulekha, and HealthGrades offer free listings that help with citation consistency. None have a paid tier worth the investment for dental clinics in most markets.

### Should I get a website from Practo?

We strongly recommend an independent provider. A Practo-built website is hosted on Practo's subdomain (yourname.practo.com) — you still build Practo's authority, not your own. And you lose the website if you stop the subscription.
