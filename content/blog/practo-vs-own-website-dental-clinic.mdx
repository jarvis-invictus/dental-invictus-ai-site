## The Question Every Dentist Is Asking

You have probably had this conversation at a dental conference or in a WhatsApp doctor group:

*"Should I pay for Practo's premium listing or build my own website? Which one actually gets me patients?"*

It is a genuinely important question — because the answer is not as obvious as either Practo's sales team or a website developer would have you believe. Both platforms have real strengths and real limitations.

After working with 50+ dental clinics across India, here is what the data actually shows.

---

## What Is Practo and How Does It Work?

Practo is India's largest healthcare marketplace. It aggregates listings for doctors, clinics, and diagnostic centres, and patients use it to search, compare, and book healthcare appointments.

For dental clinics, Practo offers:

**Free listing:**
- Basic profile with name, location, specializations, and qualifications
- Appears in Practo search results
- Patients can see your profile but cannot book directly

**Paid listings (Practo Prime/Boost):**
- Higher visibility in Practo search results
- Online appointment booking enabled
- Patient reviews and ratings system
- Analytics dashboard
- Cost: ₹8,000–₹25,000/year depending on city and plan

## When Practo Actually Helps

Practo is not worthless — it genuinely serves certain purposes well.

### 1. Credibility Verification
Patients who have heard of your clinic through referral or Instagram will often search your name on Practo to verify credentials. A complete free Practo profile with your MCI registration, qualifications, and clinic address acts as a **credibility signal** — especially for patients over 40 who are familiar with the platform.

**Recommendation:** Always maintain a free, complete Practo profile. Do not pay for Prime unless the specific numbers justify it for your city.

### 2. Specialty Discovery in Major Metros
In cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore, some patients do actively search for specialists on Practo — particularly for:
- Orthodontists
- Oral surgeons
- Paediatric dentists
- Implantologists

If you practice a niche specialty in a major metro, Practo Prime might generate meaningful leads. For general dentistry in Tier 2 cities, the ROI is typically poor.

### 3. Reviews Cross-Platform
Practo reviews appear in Google Search results when patients search your clinic name. Having reviews on Practo (in addition to Google) provides a richer results page and builds trust.

## When Practo Falls Short

### The Competitor Trap
This is Practo's biggest problem for dental clinics: **when a patient clicks on your Practo profile, they see your competitors listed right next to you.**

Practo's model is a marketplace — its business model depends on patients comparing options and booking the most reviewed, highest-rated clinic. If you have fewer reviews than the clinic across the street, Practo actively sends your patients to that competitor.

Your own website has no competitors on it. A patient who visits your website sees only your treatments, your team, your testimonials, your story.

### You Build Practo's Authority, Not Yours
When Practo ranks on Google for "dentist near me in Pune," they get the click — not you. You benefit only if the patient then finds and clicks on your specific Practo listing. But even then, the patient's next search (your name + city) will show Practo, not your own domain.

Every piece of content, every review, every booking that goes through Practo builds Practo's domain authority on Google. Your own website, by contrast, builds **your** authority. Over 2–3 years, a well-maintained website with local SEO content will outrank Practo for your specific location and treatments.

### No WhatsApp — India's #1 Conversion Channel
Practo does not integrate with WhatsApp. In India, where 73% of patients prefer WhatsApp communication, this is a significant conversion bottleneck. Patients who want to ask a quick question or confirm something before booking will bounce from Practo rather than wait for a callback.

## The Best Strategy: Use Both — But Invest in the Right One

Here is the framework we recommend for every dental clinic:

**Free Practo profile:** Always yes. Takes 30 minutes, zero cost, adds credibility and a second review source. Keep it complete and updated.

**Practo Prime/Boost:** Only if you are:
- In a major metro (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad)
- Practicing a specialist service (orthodontics, implants, oral surgery)
- Have consistently good Practo reviews (4.5+ with 30+ reviews)
- And can measure actual bookings coming from Practo

**Own website:** Always yes. This is your #1 digital asset. Build it properly, optimize it for local SEO, connect it to WhatsApp, and create treatment content monthly. Over 24 months, it will outperform any Practo subscription by a significant margin.

## FAQ

### Can I rank on Google without my own website?

You can rank in the **Google Map Pack** (the 3-listing map section) using only your Google Business Profile — no website needed. However, for organic search results and targeting specific treatment keywords, you need your own website. A GBP + website combination is significantly more powerful than either alone.

### Practo says they get millions of visitors. Why am I getting so few leads?

Practo's traffic is real. But it is divided across hundreds of thousands of doctor listings. Your individual listing visibility depends on your plan, your reviews, your specialty, and how many competing clinics are in your area. In Tier 2 cities with high competition, individual listings often receive very low impressions — often fewer than 100 per month.

### Is there a cheaper alternative to Practo for dental listings?

Yes — and most are free. Justdial, Lybrate, Sulekha, and HealthGrades all offer free listings that help with citation consistency (important for Google ranking). None of them have a paid tier worth the investment for dental clinics in most Indian markets.

### How long does it take for my own website to start generating leads?

A new website can generate leads from day 1 if it has good WhatsApp integration and is linked from your Google Business Profile. For organic Google search rankings, expect 3–6 months for treatment-specific keyword pages. The traffic is slower to build than ads — but it is permanent.

### Should I get a website from Practo or from an independent provider?

Practo offers website building as part of some premium packages. We strongly recommend an independent provider. A Practo-built website is hosted on Practo's subdomain (yourname.practo.com) — which means you still build Practo's authority, not your own. You also lose the website if you stop the subscription. An independent website on your own domain (yourclinic.in) is a permanent asset you fully own.
