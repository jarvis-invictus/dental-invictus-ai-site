## Frequently Asked Questions


  
  <FAQAccordion>
    <FAQItem question="Can I rank on Google without my own website?">
      You can rank in the Google Map Pack using only your Google Business Profile. However, for organic search results and targeting high-ticket treatments (like 'Invisalign cost Pune'), you absolutely need a conversion-optimized website.
    </FAQItem>

    <FAQItem question="Practo says they get millions of visitors. Why am I getting few leads?">
      Practo's traffic is divided across hundreds of thousands of listings. Your visibility depends on your plan, reviews, and competing clinics. If a patient searches for a dentist, Practo shows them 50 options. A custom website shows them exactly ONE option: You.
    </FAQItem>

    <FAQItem question="Is there a cheaper alternative to Practo for listings?">
      Yes — and most are free. Justdial, Lybrate, Sulekha, and Google My Business offer free listings that help with local SEO citation consistency. Google My Business is vastly more powerful than any paid Practo listing.
    </FAQItem>
